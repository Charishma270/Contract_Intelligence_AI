# Chatbot Demo Queries

## Contract Termination

- What are the termination conditions?
- Can either party terminate the agreement?
- Is there a notice period before termination?

---

## Liability

- What is the liability cap?
- Are there uncapped liabilities?
- What damages can be claimed?

---

## Renewal

- Does the contract auto-renew?
- How can renewal be stopped?
- What is the renewal term?

---

## Confidentiality

- What confidentiality obligations exist?
- How is sensitive information protected?
- Is there a non-disclosure requirement?

---

## Intellectual Property

- Who owns intellectual property created under the agreement?
- Does ownership transfer to the client?
- Are there licensing provisions?

---

## Payment

- What are the payment terms?
- Are late payment penalties included?
- How are invoice disputes handled?

---

## Risk Analysis

- What are the highest-risk clauses?
- Which clauses require legal review?
- What is the overall contract risk level?
